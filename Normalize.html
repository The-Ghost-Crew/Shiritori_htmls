Von Baeyer chemical nomenclature▶

When a ( ) or [ ] group directly follows a word ending in -cyclo (bicyclo, tricyclo, tetracyclo, pentacyclo, trioxatetracyclo, …), it's treated as a Von Baeyer ring descriptor. The brackets and the periods/commas inside them are structural and stay silent, and each main bridge count is read as one spoken quantity rather than spelled digit-by-digit.

tricyclo(2.2.1)heptane→tricyclo-two-two-one-heptane
(12.4.0)→twelve-four-zero
